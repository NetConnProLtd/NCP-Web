## Summary

FitMetric does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your data with anyone. All information you enter — food, drinks, exercise, and your goals — is stored only on your device, using Apple's on-device storage (SwiftData). FitMetric has no servers and makes no network requests.

## Apple Health (HealthKit) data

With your permission, FitMetric reads the following from Apple Health:
- Active energy burned and step count
- Heart rate
- Blood pressure
- Body weight and height
- Body Mass Index (BMI)

With your permission, FitMetric writes the following to Apple Health:
- Food and drink calories you log in the app
- Workouts you log in the app (including duration and calories burned)

**This data is used exclusively to power the app's own features** — showing your daily calorie balance, your Health dashboard readings, and keeping Apple Health in sync with what you log. It is never:
- Used for advertising, marketing, or analytics
- Sold or licensed to any third party
- Shared with any data broker
- Used to build a profile of you for any purpose outside FitMetric's own functionality

You can revoke FitMetric's access to Apple Health at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → FitMetric.

## Data storage and deletion

All data you enter into FitMetric — food entries, exercise entries, custom meals, and your goals/settings — is stored locally on your device only. It is not backed up to any server operated by the developer. If your device includes it in iCloud/device backups, that is governed by Apple's own backup and iCloud policies, not by FitMetric.

To delete all FitMetric data, delete the app from your device, or delete individual entries within the app (which also removes the corresponding entry from Apple Health, where applicable).

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

FitMetric contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking of any kind. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

## Children's privacy

FitMetric is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children, as it does not collect information from anyone.
